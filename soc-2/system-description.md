## System and services

This fictional description assumes a small organization providing internal business and customer-support services using identity services, employee endpoints, production servers, cloud file storage, network infrastructure, centralized logging, and backup/recovery services. Replace these assumptions with a validated description of the actual system before use.

## Boundaries and components

In-scope components are the people, processes, infrastructure, software, data, and supporting services that deliver the defined services. Exact locations, system names, data flows, interfaces, subservice organizations, and service commitments remain to be confirmed.

## Data and information flows

Information is created or received through authorized users and service workflows, stored in approved systems, transmitted through managed network and cloud services, monitored through security logging, and backed up under defined recovery requirements. A system-specific data-flow diagram and inventory must be attached before an examination.

## Principal service commitments and system requirements

Commitments, SLAs, security requirements, availability targets, processing-integrity requirements, confidentiality obligations, and privacy commitments must be identified from contracts, policies, and customer requirements. No numerical commitment is asserted in this sample.

## Subservice organizations and complementary controls

Cloud, hosting, telecommunications, and other service providers may be dependencies. Identify the inclusive or carve-out treatment, relevant provider controls, complementary user-entity controls, and evidence after confirming the actual service arrangement.

## Incidents and changes

Security incidents are handled under the incident response policy. System changes are subject to documented request, risk review, authorization, testing, implementation, and rollback expectations.

## Limitations and approval

This is a starter template, not a complete AICPA system description. The organization must validate system boundaries, service commitments, categories in scope, and relevant control activities. Approval and effective dates are TBD.
