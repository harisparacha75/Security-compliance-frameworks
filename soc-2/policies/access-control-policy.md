# Access Control Policy

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-002  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Policy Owner:** Information Security  
**Review Frequency:** Annually and after significant changes

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for granting, managing, reviewing, and revoking access to organizational systems and information.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, third parties, and other authorized users who access organizational systems, networks, applications, or information.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Access Provisioning
- Access must be approved by an authorized manager or system owner before it is granted.
- Access must be based on business need and the principle of least privilege.
- Each user must have a unique account wherever technically feasible.
- Access requests and approvals must be documented.

### 3.2 Authentication
- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for privileged accounts and remote access where supported.
- Passwords must meet defined complexity and length requirements.
- Default and shared credentials must be changed or disabled before systems are placed into service.
- Authentication credentials must not be shared or stored insecurely.

### 3.3 Privileged Access
- Administrative access must be limited to authorized personnel.
- Privileged accounts must be separate from standard user accounts where technically feasible.
- Privileged activities must be logged and reviewed.
- Emergency access must be authorized, documented, and reviewed after use.

### 3.4 Access Reviews
- Access rights must be reviewed at least quarterly for privileged accounts and at a defined frequency for other accounts.
- Reviews must verify that access remains appropriate for each user's role.
- Unnecessary or excessive access must be removed promptly.
- Review results and remediation actions must be documented.

### 3.5 Joiner, Mover, and Leaver Process
- New users must receive access only after authorization.
- Role or employment changes must trigger an access review and appropriate updates.
- Access must be revoked promptly upon termination or when no longer required.
- User accounts must be disabled when inactive or no longer needed, according to defined procedures.

### 3.6 Third-Party Access
- Third-party access must be approved, limited to the required scope, and time-bound where feasible.
- External accounts must have a designated internal sponsor.
- Third-party access must be reviewed periodically and removed when the engagement ends.

### 3.7 Logging and Monitoring
- Access events and privileged activities must be logged where supported.
- Logs must be protected against unauthorized modification and access.
- Suspicious access activity must be investigated and handled under the incident response process.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

- **Management:** Approves access governance requirements and supports enforcement.
- **System Owners:** Approve access and review permissions for their systems.
- **IT Operations:** Implements approved access changes and maintains access records.
- **Information Security:** Defines security requirements and monitors access-related risks.
- **Users:** Protect credentials and report suspected unauthorized access.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented with a business justification, risk assessment, compensating controls, and approval from an authorized owner. Exceptions must have an expiry or review date.

## 6. Compliance and Review

Violations must be reported and handled under applicable organizational procedures. This policy must be reviewed annually and when significant changes occur.
