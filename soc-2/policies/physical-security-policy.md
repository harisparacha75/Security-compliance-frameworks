# Physical Security Policy

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-010  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Policy Owner:** IT Operations  
**Review Frequency:** Annually and after significant changes

## 1. Purpose and scope

Sets requirements for physical access to facilities and equipment and for secure disposal of equipment, recognizing that the facility is provider-managed.

## 2. Requirements

1. Rely on the facility provider for building access control, monitoring, and environmental protections, and obtain and review the provider's assurance reports at least annually.
2. Keep in-scope equipment in locked rooms or racks provided by the facility, with access limited to authorized personnel.
3. Review the list of people with access to in-scope equipment quarterly and remove access that is no longer needed.
4. Protect laptops and removable media taken off premises with encryption and remote wipe where available.
5. Sanitize or destroy storage in equipment before disposal, return, or re-use, and keep certificates or records of sanitization.
6. Escort visitors and contractors in areas holding in-scope equipment and record their visits.
7. Report lost or stolen equipment immediately so incident response can start.

## 3. Roles and review

The policy owner maintains this policy, assigns responsibilities, and reviews it annually and after significant change. Exceptions are documented, time-limited, risk-assessed, and approved by the policy owner and IT Security.
