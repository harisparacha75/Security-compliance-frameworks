# Code of Conduct

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-012  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Policy Owner:** Management  
**Review Frequency:** Annually and after significant changes

## 1. Purpose and scope

Sets expected standards of integrity, ethical behavior, and security responsibility for personnel and contractors.

## 2. Requirements

1. Act honestly and ethically, avoid conflicts of interest, and disclose any that arise.
2. Protect confidential information and use systems only as permitted by the Acceptable Use and Information Security policies.
3. Complete security awareness training within 30 days of joining and annually afterwards.
4. Report suspected security events, policy violations, or unethical conduct promptly through the designated channels, without fear of retaliation.
5. Acknowledge this code at onboarding and annually.
6. Accept that violations may lead to disciplinary action in line with the documented process, up to termination or contract end.
7. Managers set the example and respond to reports fairly and promptly.

## 3. Roles and review

The policy owner maintains this policy, assigns responsibilities, and reviews it annually and after significant change. Exceptions are documented, time-limited, risk-assessed, and approved by the policy owner and IT Security.
