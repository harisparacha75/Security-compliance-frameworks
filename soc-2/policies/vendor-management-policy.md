# Vendor Management Policy

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-005  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Policy Owner:** Vendor Management  
**Review Frequency:** Annually and after significant changes

## 1. Purpose and scope

Sets how third parties that process in-scope information or support the in-scope system are assessed, contracted, and monitored.

## 2. Requirements

1. Maintain a vendor register with service, owner, data held, tier, contract end date, and last review date.
2. Assign each vendor a tier (Critical, Important, or Standard) before onboarding based on data sensitivity and service dependency.
3. Complete due diligence proportionate to tier before use: security questionnaire and independent assurance reports (for example a SOC 2 report or ISO/IEC 27001 certificate) for Critical and Important vendors.
4. Include security, confidentiality, incident notification (within 72 hours), sub-processor, and data return or deletion terms in contracts with Critical and Important vendors.
5. Review Critical vendors annually and Important vendors every two years, including assurance report exceptions and complementary user entity controls.
6. Record vendor risks and issues in the risk register and track remediation.
7. Revoke vendor access and confirm data return or deletion when the relationship ends.

## 3. Roles and review

The policy owner maintains this policy, assigns responsibilities, and reviews it annually and after significant change. Exceptions are documented, time-limited, risk-assessed, and approved by the policy owner and IT Security.
