# Risk Management Policy

**Document ID:** SOC2-POL-011  
**Version:** 0.1  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Policy Owner:** IT Security  
**Review Frequency:** Annually and after significant changes

## 1. Purpose and scope

Sets how security objectives are defined and how risks, fraud and misuse scenarios, and significant changes are assessed and treated.

## 2. Requirements

1. Define measurable security objectives annually and review progress at least quarterly.
2. Assess risks at least annually using a documented methodology with likelihood and impact scales, and record them in a risk register with owners.
3. Include fraud and misuse scenarios, such as insider abuse of access, in each assessment.
4. Trigger a risk review for significant changes to systems, vendors, personnel, laws, or the threat environment.
5. Choose and record a treatment (mitigate, avoid, transfer, or accept) for each risk; management approves acceptance of High and Critical residual risks.
6. Assess business disruption risks and link them to continuity plans.
7. Report risk status to management at least quarterly.

## 3. Roles and review

The policy owner maintains this policy, assigns responsibilities, and reviews it annually and after significant change. Exceptions are documented, time-limited, risk-assessed, and approved by the policy owner and IT Security.
