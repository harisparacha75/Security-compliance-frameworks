# Illustrative ISMS Scope

**Document ID:** ISMS-SCOPE-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Review Frequency:** Annually and after significant changes

## 1. Scope Statement

For this fictional portfolio scenario, the Information Security Management System (ISMS) scope covers the people, processes, information, endpoints, identity services, cloud file storage, production servers, network infrastructure, and supporting IT operations used to deliver a small organization's internal business and customer-support services. It includes the sample assets named in the risk register and the controls represented in the accompanying registers.

## 2. Boundaries and Interfaces

The scenario assumes that cloud service providers and other suppliers are external dependencies. Their controls are not treated as implemented by this portfolio. Product engineering and customer-operated environments are outside this illustrative scope unless later added through a documented scope review. Building-level physical security is treated as a supplier-managed dependency; the related Annex A physical controls remain `Pending assessment` until supplier assurance and in-scope equipment handling are reviewed.

## 3. Scope Review

A real organization must confirm organizational boundaries, locations, services, interested-party requirements, interfaces, and exclusions before approving an ISMS scope. This fictional scope is not an organizational determination.
