# Supplier Security Standard

**Document ID:** ISO-STD-002  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Owner:** Vendor Management  
**Review Cycle:** Annually and after significant change

## 1. Purpose and scope

Set security expectations for suppliers that process in-scope information or provide services the organization depends on, including cloud, hosting, managed service, and facility providers.

## 2. Supplier tiering

| Tier | Criteria | Review frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Holds Confidential or Restricted information, or an outage would breach a Tier 1 recovery objective | Annual |
| Important | Holds Internal information or supports Tier 2 services | Every two years |
| Standard | No in-scope information and no material dependency | At renewal |

## 3. Due diligence before onboarding

1. Vendor Management and IT Security assign a tier.
2. Obtain security evidence proportionate to tier: independent assurance reports or certifications (for example ISO/IEC 27001 certificate or SOC 2 report), a completed security questionnaire, and a summary of sub-processors.
3. IT Security reviews results, records gaps, and approves, conditions, or rejects the supplier. Critical suppliers need Management approval.

## 4. Contract requirements

Contracts with Critical and Important suppliers include: confidentiality; data protection and location; access controls; security incident notification without undue delay and within 72 hours at most; right to receive assurance reports; sub-processor approval; change notification; availability commitments; support for exit; and return or deletion of data at termination (A.5.20).

## 5. Ongoing monitoring

Review assurance reports, service performance, incidents, and material changes at the review frequency above (A.5.22). Record issues and follow up with the supplier. For the facility provider, review physical security and environmental assurance because the organization does not operate those controls directly (RISK-017).

## 6. Offboarding

Revoke supplier access, retrieve or confirm deletion of data, and update the supplier register and asset inventory.

## 7. Supplier register

Maintain a register with supplier name, services, tier, data held, owner, contract end date, last review date, and assurance evidence reference.
