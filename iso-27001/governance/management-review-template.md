# Management Review Template

**Document ID:** ISO-GOV-005  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example — no review performed  
**Owner:** Management  
**Review Cycle:** At least annually

Use this template to run and record the management review required by clause 9.3. Complete it with actual data; do not pre-fill outcomes.

## Standing agenda and inputs (clause 9.3.2)

1. Status of actions from previous management reviews.
2. Changes in external and internal issues and interested-party requirements ([context register](context-and-interested-parties-register.csv)).
3. Information security performance: nonconformities and corrective actions ([register](nonconformity-corrective-action-register.csv)); monitoring and measurement results; audit results; fulfilment of [objectives](security-objectives-register.csv).
4. Feedback from interested parties.
5. Results of risk assessment and status of the risk treatment plan; residual risks awaiting acceptance.
6. Resource adequacy.
7. Opportunities for continual improvement.
