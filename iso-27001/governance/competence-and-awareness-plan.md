# Competence and Awareness Plan

**Document ID:** ISO-GOV-003  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Owner:** HR  
**Review Cycle:** Annually

Defines the competence required for security-relevant roles, how it is evaluated, and how awareness is delivered (clauses 7.2 and 7.3; control A.6.3).

## Role competence matrix

| Role | Competence requirement | Evaluation method | Training or action | Record | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IT Security | Risk assessment, control governance, incident coordination | Role review and practical assessment | Role-specific training | Training and review records | Management |
| SOC | Monitoring, triage, evidence handling, incident escalation | Scenario exercise and supervisor review | Detection and response training | Exercise records | SOC |
| IT Operations | Secure configuration, change, backup and recovery | Technical review and restore test results | Operations training | Test and training records | IT Operations |
| Asset and risk owners | Risk ownership, access approvals, control responsibilities | Annual role review | Security responsibilities briefing | Briefing attendance | Management |
| All personnel | Policy awareness, phishing recognition, event reporting | Quiz and phishing simulations | Onboarding within 30 days and annual refresher | Completion records | IT Security |

## Awareness content

Acceptable use, information classification and handling, phishing and social engineering, MFA use, reporting security events, and clear desk and screen rules. Content is updated at least annually and after significant incidents.

## Effectiveness

Review completion rates (OBJ-005), phishing simulation results, and incident trends each quarter; address gaps with targeted training.
