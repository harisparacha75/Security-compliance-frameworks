# Information Security Communication Plan

**Document ID:** ISO-GOV-002  
**Version:** 0.2  
**Status:** Draft — Portfolio Example  
**Owner:** IT Security  
**Review Cycle:** Annually

Defines what is communicated, when, to whom, by whom, and through which channel (clause 7.4). Confirm legal and contractual requirements before any external notification.

| Communication | Audience | Trigger or frequency | Owner | Channel | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Information security policy and updates | Personnel and relevant contractors | On issue, on change, and at onboarding | IT Security | Approved internal channel | Acknowledgments |
| Security objectives and performance | Management and control owners | Quarterly | IT Security | Review meeting and report | Minutes and reports |
| Security incidents and alerts | Response team and affected stakeholders | Per incident; updates every two hours during continuity plan activation | SOC | Incident channel | Incident record |
| How to report a security event | All personnel | Onboarding and annually | SOC | Awareness training and intranet | Training records |
| Supplier security requirements | Relevant suppliers | Onboarding and material change | Vendor Management | Contract and vendor portal | Supplier acknowledgment |
| Customer notifications | Affected customers | Per approved incident decision and contract terms | Management | Approved customer channel | Notification record |
| Authority notifications | Regulators and law enforcement as required | Per legal decision | Legal/Compliance | Approved channel | Notification record |
