# Information Security Communication Plan

**Status:** Draft template — not approved

| Communication | Audience | Trigger/frequency | Owner | Channel | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Information security policy and updates | Personnel and relevant contractors | On issue/change and onboarding | IT Security | Approved internal channel | Acknowledgments |
| Security objectives and performance | Management and control owners | Quarterly | IT Security | Review meeting/report | Minutes and reports |
| Security incidents and alerts | Response team and affected stakeholders | Per incident | SOC/Incident Lead | Incident channel | Incident record |
| Supplier security requirements | Relevant suppliers | Onboarding and material change | Vendor Management | Contract/vendor portal | Supplier acknowledgment |
| External notifications | Contractual/regulatory parties as required | Per approved incident/legal decision | Legal/Management | Approved channel | Notification record |

Communications must identify what is communicated, when, to whom, by whom, and through which channel. Confirm legal and contractual requirements before external notification.
