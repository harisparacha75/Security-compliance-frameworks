## Program requirements (clause 9.2)

Define audit scope, criteria, frequency, methods, responsibilities, impartiality safeguards, reporting, and follow-up. Auditors must not audit their own work. Prioritize areas by risk, recent change, and prior audit results. Cover every clause 4–10 requirement and every applicable Annex A control within each three-year cycle.

## Audit record minimums

Record objectives, scope, criteria, evidence sampled, findings, conclusions, auditor, auditee response, corrective action, and closure verification. No audit is represented as completed.
