
# Incident Response Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes a process for identifying, reporting, responding to, and recovering from information security incidents.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, information systems, networks, applications, and data managed by the organization.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 Incident Identification
- Security incidents must be identified and reported promptly.
- Potential incidents include unauthorized access, malware infections, data breaches, and service disruptions.

### 3.2 Incident Reporting
- Employees must report suspected security incidents to the designated security team.
- All reported incidents must be recorded in an incident register.

### 3.3 Incident Response
- Incidents must be classified according to severity and potential business impact.
- The response team must investigate, contain, and mitigate incidents.
- Evidence must be preserved to support investigation and analysis.

### 3.4 Recovery and Lessons Learned
- Affected systems must be restored securely.
- The incident response team must document the root cause and corrective actions.
- Lessons learned must be used to improve security controls.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Security Team | Investigate and respond to incidents |
| IT Team | Contain threats and restore systems |
| Management | Approve major response actions |
| Employees | Report suspected incidents |

## 5. Compliance

Failure to comply with this policy may result in corrective action under organizational procedures.
