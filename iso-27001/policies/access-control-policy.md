# Access Control Policy

**Document ID:** POL-AC-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Sample - For Portfolio Use  
**Review Cycle:** Annually  

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the requirements for managing access to information systems, applications, and data. Its purpose is to reduce the risk of unauthorized access and protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, administrators, and other authorized users who access organizational information systems and data.

## 3. Policy Requirements

### 3.1 User Access Management

- Each user must have a unique account.
- Access must be granted based on job responsibilities and business needs.
- Access must follow the principle of least privilege.
- Shared accounts should be avoided unless formally approved and controlled.

### 3.2 Authentication

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA) must be enabled for privileged accounts and remote access.
- Passwords must meet the organization's defined complexity and length requirements.
- Authentication credentials must not be shared or stored insecurely.

### 3.3 Privileged Access

- Administrative access must be restricted to authorized personnel.
- Privileged accounts must be reviewed regularly.
- Administrative activities should be logged and monitored.

### 3.4 Access Reviews

- User access must be reviewed at least quarterly.
- Unnecessary or excessive permissions must be removed.
- Access changes must be documented.

### 3.5 Joiner, Mover, and Leaver Process

- New users must receive access only after authorization.
- Access must be updated when job responsibilities change.
- Accounts must be disabled promptly when a user's employment or engagement ends.

## 4. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management | Approves access control requirements |
| IT Administrator | Implements and maintains access permissions |
| Information Security | Monitors access controls and reviews |
| System Owner | Approves access to assigned systems |
| User | Protects credentials and follows this policy |

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented, justified, approved by the appropriate authority, and reviewed periodically.

## 6. Monitoring and Compliance

Access control activities should be monitored through logs, periodic access reviews, and internal assessments. Policy violations must be reported and handled according to organizational procedures.

## 7. Review and Approval

This policy should be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in systems, risks, or business requirements.
