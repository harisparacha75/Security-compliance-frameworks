# Information Security Policy

**Document ID:** ISP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Review Frequency:** Annually

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the principles and responsibilities for protecting information assets and maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to employees, contractors, third parties, information systems, networks, applications, and data within the organization's defined ISMS scope.

## 3. Policy Objectives

- Protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and loss.
- Establish clear security responsibilities.
- Support risk-based security controls.
- Ensure compliance with applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
- Maintain business continuity and incident response capabilities.

## 4. Information Security Requirements

### 4.1 Risk Management
Information security risks must be identified, assessed, documented, and treated according to the organization's risk management process.

### 4.2 Access Control
Access to information and systems must be authorized, restricted according to business needs, and reviewed periodically.

### 4.3 Asset Management
Information assets must be identified, assigned an owner, classified, and handled according to their sensitivity.

### 4.4 Incident Management
Security incidents must be reported, recorded, investigated, and addressed through a documented incident management process.

### 4.5 Data Protection
Information must be protected throughout its lifecycle, including storage, transmission, use, and disposal.

### 4.6 Business Continuity
Critical information and systems must be supported by appropriate backup, recovery, and continuity arrangements.

### 4.7 Supplier Security
Relevant information security risks associated with suppliers and third parties must be assessed and managed.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Management | Approves the policy and provides resources. |
| Information Security Manager | Coordinates implementation and monitoring. |
| Asset Owners | Ensure appropriate protection of assigned assets. |
| Employees | Follow security requirements and report incidents. |

## 6. Compliance

Violations of this policy must be reported and handled according to organizational procedures.

## 7. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented, risk-assessed, approved by an authorized person, and reviewed periodically.

## 8. Review and Approval

This policy must be reviewed at least annually or when significant changes occur.
