# Acceptable Use Policy

**Document ID:** AUP-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Draft  
**Owner:** Information Security Manager  
**Review Frequency:** Annually

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of organizational information assets, systems, devices, networks, and services to protect information and reduce security risks.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and third parties who access or use organizational information assets and systems.

## 3. Acceptable Use Requirements

All users must:

- Use organizational systems and information assets for authorized business purposes.
- Protect their accounts, passwords, and authentication credentials.
- Follow information security policies and procedures.
- Store and share information using approved systems and services.
- Report suspected security incidents, data loss, or unauthorized access promptly.
- Lock devices when unattended and protect them from unauthorized access.
- Use organizational resources responsibly and comply with applicable laws.

## 4. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Access information or systems without authorization.
- Share passwords or authentication credentials with others.
- Install unauthorized software or bypass security controls.
- Disable antivirus, endpoint protection, or other security mechanisms.
- Use organizational systems to distribute malicious software or conduct unauthorized security testing.
- Transfer confidential information to unauthorized external services or personal accounts.
- Access, download, or distribute illegal or inappropriate content using organizational resources.
- Connect unauthorized devices to organizational networks.

## 5. Email and Internet Usage

- Users must remain alert to phishing emails, suspicious links, and malicious attachments.
- Organizational email must be used in accordance with security and data protection requirements.
- Sensitive information must only be transmitted through approved channels.
- Users must not use organizational email to send unauthorized or confidential information to external recipients.

## 6. Device and Remote Access Security

- Organizational devices must be protected with appropriate authentication and security controls.
- Remote access must use approved secure connections and authentication mechanisms.
- Lost or stolen devices must be reported promptly.
- Users must not allow unauthorized individuals to use organizational devices.

## 7. Data Handling

- Information must be handled according to its classification and sensitivity.
- Confidential information must only be shared with authorized recipients.
- Information must not be copied to unauthorized removable media or personal storage.
- Sensitive information must be securely disposed of when no longer required.

## 8. Monitoring and Compliance

Organizational systems may be monitored in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, and approved organizational procedures.

Violations of this policy must be reported and handled through established disciplinary and incident management procedures.

## 9. Exceptions

Exceptions must be documented, risk-assessed, approved by an authorized person, and reviewed periodically.

## 10. Review and Approval

This policy must be reviewed annually or when significant changes occur in technology, business operations, or security requirements.
